ALJ Insights Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ALJ Insights mobile application for Android and iOS (the "App") collects, uses, shares, protects and deletes your personal information, and the choices and rights you have.

The App is operated by Abdul Latif Jameel IPR Company Limited, a limited company registered in Jersey under company number 111739, whose registered office is at 15 Esplanade, St Helier, Jersey JE1 1RB ("Abdul Latif Jameel", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for personal information processed through the App.

In short
  • We collect the information needed to run your account, personalise your news and keep the App working: your email and name, your preferences, how you use the App, and technical and crash data.
  • We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or use your device's advertising ID.
  • We use a small number of service providers (listed below) that process data on our behalf.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the App (see how).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. When you create an account with email, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never visible to us.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook. If you choose one of these, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from that provider. We ask Facebook only for your public profile and email address. We do not receive your password for those services.
  • Guest use. If you continue as a guest, we create an anonymous account identifier so your preferences can be saved. No email or name is collected.
  • Preferences. The topics and business sectors you follow, your language, your notification setting and whether you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • Messages to us. If you use the feedback / get-in-touch form, we collect the name, email address, phone number and message you enter.

Information collected as you use the App

  • App activity. Articles and videos you view, like, bookmark, share or add to a playlist, the actions you take in the App, and the search terms you enter (including searches made by voice, see Device permissions). These are linked to your account so the App can show your saved items and relevant content.
  • Analytics. Screens viewed and in-app events, such as searches and taps, with the App language. These are used to understand how the App is used and to improve it.
  • Device and push notification identifiers. An app-instance identifier used by our analytics provider, and a push notification subscription identifier and token used to send you notifications.
  • Crash and performance data. If the App crashes or runs into an error, we receive a diagnostic report. This includes the device model, operating system version, App version, the error details and performance timings.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, your contacts, your calendar, your SMS or call logs, your files, or any payment or financial information.

2. Device permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature, and you can refuse or withdraw them at any time in your device settings:

  • Camera and photos. These let you choose or take a profile picture. The picture is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, and it is removed from the App when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. These let you search by voice. Your speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition service (provided by Google on Android and by Apple on iOS, under their own privacy terms). The App receives only the resulting text, which is handled like any other search term. We do not record or store audio.
  • Notifications. These let us send you news alerts. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • create and manage your account and let you sign in (performance of our agreement with you);
  • provide the App's features, such as your followed topics, bookmarks, likes, playlists, search, audio and video content and notifications (performance of our agreement with you);
  • respond to messages and support requests you send us (legitimate interests / your request);
  • send you the newsletter, only if you have opted in (consent);
  • understand how the App is used, fix problems, and improve its content and performance (legitimate interests);
  • keep the App and its users secure, and prevent misuse (legitimate interests);
  • comply with legal obligations (legal obligation).

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. When we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Apart from the service providers above, we share it only:

  • with other Abdul Latif Jameel group companies, where needed to respond to an enquiry you send us or to operate the App, under this Privacy Policy;
  • if required by law, regulation or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Abdul Latif Jameel, our users or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of all or part of our business, in which case this Privacy Policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Advertising and tracking

The App contains no advertising. It does not access your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Security

Information sent between the App and our servers and service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Access to personal information is limited to authorised staff and providers who need it, and passwords are stored in hashed form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

8. Data retention

  • Account, preference and activity data is kept for as long as your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account (see below).
  • Messages you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your request, and deleted with your account.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for limited periods set in our analytics and crash-reporting services, after which they are automatically deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify you.
  • We may keep limited information for longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed to resolve disputes or enforce our terms. Residual copies in backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your ALJ Insights account and its associated data at any time, directly in the App:

  1. Open the App and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account record and the data linked to it: your preferences and followed topics, likes, bookmarks, playlists, shares, article views, search history, activity logs and messages you sent through the App. We also end your sessions. If you signed in with Apple, Google or Facebook, we revoke the App's access to that account. Your profile picture and saved sign-in details are removed from your device.

If you cannot access the App, you can ask us to delete your account by emailing dataprotection@alj.ae from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "ALJ Insights account deletion request". We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days. Guest accounts contain no name or email; you can remove their data by uninstalling the App.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete your information (see section 9);
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, meaning a copy of information you provided, in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it (for example, the newsletter), without affecting earlier processing;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact dataprotection@alj.ae. You can also turn off notifications, unsubscribe from the newsletter and change your topics in the App at any time, and withdraw device permissions in your device settings.

11. International transfers

Abdul Latif Jameel and our service providers operate in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United Arab Emirates. Where the law requires it, we protect such transfers with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Children

The App is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and when you create an account you are asked to confirm that you are of legal age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or when the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the App or by email before they take effect.
